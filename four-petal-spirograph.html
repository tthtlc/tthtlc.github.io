Spirograph study / 04

Four-petal spirograph

Geometry

Four petals, each drawn in its own frame rotated by θ = k·π/2. The envelope ρ(u) = sin(πu) rises from the base to the petal tip. Inside the local frame a lateral sway w·ρ·sin(2πu) and a small loop of radius d·ρ, rotating at phase φ = 2π·N·u, are added.

x(u) = ρ·(cos θ·(1 + d·cos φ) − sin θ·(w·sin 2πu + d·sin φ))
y(u) = ρ·(sin θ·(1 + d·cos φ) + cos θ·(w·sin 2πu + d·sin φ))
ρ = sin πu, φ = 2π·N·u, θ = k·π/2, k = 0…3

Every slider move rewrites the formula.
