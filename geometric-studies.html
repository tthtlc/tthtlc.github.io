Twenty Geometric Studies

Circles, recursive polygons, harmonic waves, woven ribbons and parametric curves. Every panel is generated by its own JavaScript drawing function—not a stored image. Change the controls, inspect a larger rendering, or export a high-resolution PNG.

20 procedural artworks

Open this file directly in a modern browser; no installation, libraries, or network connection is required. “Expand” renders an 800 × 800 study. PNG export renders at 1600 × 1600. The star tessellation and fivefold mosaic are ornamental constructions rather than exact historical tilings or a strict Penrose tiling.
