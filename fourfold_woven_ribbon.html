Parametric reconstruction / 01

Four-fold woven ribbon

Geometry & derivation

This is a reconstruction, not a uniquely identified source equation.

Move around a circle C(t)=R(cos t,sin t). Add a rapidly oscillating displacement of amplitude r along a direction that turns backwards: D(t)=(cos t,−sin t).

u = R cos t + r cos(Nt+φ) cos t
v = R sin t − r cos(Nt+φ) sin t

Rotate the result through α: x=u cos α−v sin α; y=u sin α+v cos α.

Equivalently z=e^(iα)[R e^(it)+r cos(Nt+φ)e^(−it)]. The slow two-parameter ribbon is F(t,s)=e^(iα)[R e^(it)+s e^(−it)], −r≤s≤r. Its boundary curves are ellipses, and its fold condition is s=R cos(2t).

Integer N closes at 2π. Choosing N=4k+2 gives exact quarter-turn symmetry. The preset N=102 creates 102 transverse oscillations per circuit. This is a planar woven curve, not proof of a 3D surface or knot.

Ten copies are drawn at once, each offset in centre radius R by 0.2. The pulse alternates between contracting (R, R−0.2, …) and expanding (R, R+0.2, …).
