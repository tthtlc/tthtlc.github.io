Epicycloid + epicycloid

Geometry

A fixed five-lobed epicycloid (200·cos t − 96·cos 5t) is added point by point to a standard epicycloid of radius R + r.

x(t) = 200·cos t − 96·cos 5t + (R + r)·cos t − d·cos((R + r)/r · t)
y(t) = 200·sin t − 96·sin 5t + (R + r)·sin t − d·sin((R + r)/r · t)
