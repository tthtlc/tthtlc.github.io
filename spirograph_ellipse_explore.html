Rolling curve / 19

Elliptical hypotrochoid

Geometry

A circle of radius r rolls inside a fixed ellipse with semi-axes A and B. Its centre travels the ellipse of radii A − r and B − r, and a pen fixed to the rolling circle traces the curve.

x(t) = (A − r)·cos t + r·cos(k·t)
y(t) = (B − r)·sin t − r·sin(k·t)
k = (A + B)/(2·r) − 1
