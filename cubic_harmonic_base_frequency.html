Cubic harmonic curve

Geometry

A unit-circle motion run at angular frequency b, minus a cubed, high-frequency harmonic. Angles are in radians; the trace runs from 0 to 2π.

x(t) = cos(b·t) − [cos(nt)]³
y(t) = sin(b·t) − [sin(nt)]³

b runs from 0 to 12 in steps of 0.1, so the base loop may take several turns (or barely advance). Frequency n and power p are integers; amplitude A scales both subtracted terms. The trace closes on itself only when b is a whole number. Animate steps the amplitude A from 0 to 2 in 0.05 increments, then back, turning the whole curve 2° per step up to 360°, then back. Spiral overlays the drawing at 10° steps through a full turn.
