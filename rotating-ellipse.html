The 5:1 Ellipse

An ellipse with a constant major axis spins about its center, while its minor axis oscillates between two values — at a rate you set, measured in cycles per revolution.

b(θ) = (b1 + b2) / 2 + (b2 − b1) / 2 · cos nθ

The orange trace follows the tip of the minor axis and never fades: because b completes n cycles per revolution, it inscribes an n-petal flower (when n is a whole number) while the major axis a stays fixed.
