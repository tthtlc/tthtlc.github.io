The 5:1 Ellipse

Geometry

An ellipse with a constant semi-major axis a rotates about its centre, while its semi-minor axis b oscillates between two values at n cycles per revolution.

b(θ) = (b₁+b₂)/2 + (b₂−b₁)/2 · cos(n·θ)

The orange locus is the path of the minor-axis tip over one full turn — an n-petal flower when n is a whole number.
