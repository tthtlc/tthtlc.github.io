Hyperbolic checkerboard

Geometry & terminology

This saddle is a hyperbolic paraboloid, not the constant-curvature hyperbolic plane.

S(u,v) = (u, v, k·u·v)

Holding either parameter fixed gives a straight line. Two crossing families of these lines bound four-sided cells, colored by the parity of their grid indices.

These cells are quadrilaterals, not generally intrinsic squares. Each colored cell follows the curved surface. The saddle has variable negative Gaussian curvature when k ≠ 0.

Surface & appearance

Two families of straight rulings form a checkerboard on the saddle. Drag for an edge-on view to see the criss-crossing lines. Exports are created locally; no external libraries or network connection are needed.
