Animation

Animate 2 loads the selected saved PNG's fx, fy, sine power and amplitude divisor, then sweeps the divisor 0.01 at a time: up to divisor + 2.0, down to divisor − 2.0, then up again, cycling. Values stay inside the slider's 0.5–6 range.
