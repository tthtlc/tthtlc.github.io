Parametric curve / Cubic harmonics

Cubic harmonic amplitude sweep

Geometry

A unit-circle motion minus a cubed, high-frequency harmonic. Angles are in radians; the trace runs from 0 to 2π.

x(t) = cos(t) − A·[cos(nt)]^p
y(t) = sin(t) − A·[sin(nt)]^p

Frequency and power are integers. The amplitude A steps between the min and max levels in 0.05 increments, then back. Each step also turns the whole curve 2° clockwise up to 360°, then 2° anticlockwise back to 0°.
