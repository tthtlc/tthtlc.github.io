Outward water waves

A continuous source sends circular crests across a three-dimensional water surface.

η(r,t) = A · E(r) · cos(kr − ωt)
k = 2π/λ   ω = 2πf
Model and controls

Drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Lengths and time are in illustrative units. Frequency and wavelength are independently adjustable; this is a kinematic demonstration, not a fluid solver or a gravity–capillary dispersion model.

To avoid a singularity at the source, the envelope is smoothed: E(r) = [1 + (r/r₀)²]⁻¹/⁴, with r₀ = 1. It approaches r⁻¹/² far away. This smooth core is illustrative, not the exact source solution. Disabling spreading sets E = 1. The source is already running throughout the displayed surface at t = 0.
