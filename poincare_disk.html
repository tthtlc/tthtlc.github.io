What stays invariant?

The animation applies a disk-preserving Möbius isometry to every point. Shapes look smaller near the rim, but hyperbolic distances and angles remain unchanged.

Geodesics are circle arcs meeting the boundary at right angles, or diameters. The boundary is infinitely far away; it is not part of the plane.

ds² = 4(dx² + dy²)/(1 − x² − y²)²

The grid has equal hyperbolic radial spacing. For curvature −1, triangle area equals π minus its angle sum (in radians).
