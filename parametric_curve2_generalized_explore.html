Parametric curve 2 — generalized

Geometry

The figure is one parametric curve drawn for t in [−c, c], then rotated four times by 90° to form a symmetric rosette.

x(t) = a · sin²(−b·t) · 2cos(cos(d·t))
y(t) = a · sin(sin(−b·t)) · cos²(d·t)

Colour encodes distance from the origin: warm and bright at the centre, cooling and dimming toward the rim. Animate sweeps bbbb up and down around its current value.
